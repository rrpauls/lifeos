# Protocol: annual review

Run at year-end (December), after the Q4 quarter review flags it.

1. Read all four quarterly reviews for the year (`reviews/quarterly/YYYY-Q1..Q4`)
2. Read the annual objectives in `goals/year.md` and score each — % met, one honest
   paragraph. (Scored by how the year's quarterly KRs delivered them — there's no separate
   annual KR list; objectives are delivered by the quarters' KRs.) If never set, say so plainly.
3. Read the year's arc from the four quarters' state/energy paragraphs — one paragraph on the
   year's trajectory (did the base hold across the year? the floors and the recoveries?). For
   a recovery/wellbeing year, this arc *is* the headline, ahead of the objectives.
4. Pull the top 3–5 durable patterns of the year from `meta/reflections.md`.
5. Name the year in one line — what it was, honestly.
6. Write `reviews/annual/YYYY.md`.
7. Draft next year's annual objectives in `goals/year.md` (archive the old one) — one or two
   objectives the quarters will ladder up to. Keep it small and true.
8. Propose edits to AGENTS.md (Life Areas, Season, operating principles) based on what the
   year taught — present as "Here's what I'd change and why." Approve before writing.
9. Log approved changes to `meta/changelog.md`.

Notes:
- The annual objectives are the top of the cascade: **day → week → month → quarter → year.**
  Surfaced monthly (month review), scored quarterly (quarter review), set/closed here.
- Honesty above all. A year that missed its objectives but rebuilt a life is not a failed
  year — read it the way the recovery-season rule reads a quarter.
