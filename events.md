# Events

One-time and recurring events (birthdays, anniversaries, key dates). Most tie to people in
`context/people.md`. Surfaced **daily** (morning: today + opening nudge windows) and **weekly**
(Monday: the coming 7 days). See `.claude/protocols/events.md`.

**Format:** `MM-DD` (recurring) or `YYYY-MM-DD` (one-time) · event · who · nudge (days before).
